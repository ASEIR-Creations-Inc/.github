# ASEIR Creations Inc

**Engineering for traders and small businesses.**

We design, build and operate the software around trading and retail — alert systems that show

tooling for shops that need to work offline and stay honest.

## What we do

| | |
|---|---|
| **Custom trading alerts** | Your rules turned into a scanner that fires inside Telegram. Each alert is a full chart of the setup — entry, stop, fill, slippage — in one image. No TradingView tab-hopping. |
| **Exchange integration** | Your system wired to a demo account first, then a live account on your approval. Brackets, slippage guards, position sizing, margin checks. |
| **Verifiable backtesting** | Trades you can inspect one by one. Each chart carries a settings stamp you can recompute yourself with our open-source verifier. |
| **Account health monitoring** | Equity, margin, fees, funding, drawdown — reported on Telegram on your schedule, with immediate alerts on anything abnormal. |
| **Retail software** | Offline-first point of sale with a tamper-proof audit trail, built for shops that can't afford downtime or dishonest records. |
